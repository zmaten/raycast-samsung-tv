# Samsung TV Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Turn the TV on (Wake-on-LAN), off, or toggle power
- Open, switch and close TV apps; switch to Live TV, HDMI, Source and Home
- TV Remote: arrows, OK, back, home, playback, volume and typing with keyboard shortcuts
- Play YouTube on TV from the browser tab, clipboard or a pasted link
- Use the TV as an extended Mac display over AirPlay
- Finds the TV on the network automatically; Select TV for homes with several TVs
- Follows the TV when its IP address changes
- MAC address and AirPlay name learned from the TV automatically
